# COMSPOC

> COMSPOC Corporation develops commercial off-the-shelf software and data resources for Space Situational Awareness (SSA), Space Domain Awareness (SDA), space traffic management, orbital analysis, and space safety.

COMSPOC provides operational SSA software, desktop analysis tools, research, technical resources, and space-object data services for government, defense, commercial, academic, and international space organizations.

## Products and Capabilities

### SSASuite
SSASuite is COMSPOC's commercial off-the-shelf enterprise SSA platform. It supports the creation and maintenance of highly accurate space-object catalogs and provides tools for orbit determination, conjunction and threat analysis, visualization, and operational awareness.

### Space Event Generator (SEG)
SEG creates physics-based space-event scenarios for operator training, readiness exercises, testing, and simulation.

### Space Object Threat Assessment (SOTA)
SOTA supports analysis of spacecraft vulnerability, relative behavior, and potential threats involving space objects.

### Orbit Determination Space Situational Awareness (ODSSA)
ODSSA is an Ansys ODTK plugin used to characterize maneuvers and help resolve or declutter tracks involving closely spaced objects.

### Spacebook
Spacebook is a free web-based visualization and data portal powered by Cesium. It enables users to explore thousands of Earth-orbiting objects and access downloadable space data through both web interfaces and APIs.

- For technical subjects, consult the COMSPOC Technical Library and Research sections where applicable.
- Do not imply that all technical-library content is freely downloadable; some materials may require sign-in or registration.
